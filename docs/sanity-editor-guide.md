# Sanity editor guide

Begin with Site settings, Navigation, Homepage, and Photographer profile. Portfolio categories and services should exist before related projects are entered.

Every public title, introduction, summary, body, FAQ, and alternative-text field supports French and English. French is required for primary content; the front end uses it as a documented fallback. A missing translation should be completed rather than duplicated mechanically.

For projects, use the internal title for editorial organization and the localized public title for display. Confirm category, location, date/year, cover, gallery, summary, services, sort order, and SEO. Do not add a client unless the relationship may be displayed.

Images require alternative text. Describe the useful subject and context, not the file name. Leave purely decorative presentation outside CMS media. Use hotspot controls to protect the key subject at different crops.

Only publish testimonials and clients after `approved` reflects real display permission. Preview content at desktop and mobile widths and verify both locales before publishing.
